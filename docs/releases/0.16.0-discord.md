## 🎉 Jellystack 0.16.0 + TV Beta 6 — Jellyfin 12 ready

Jellyfin 12 is out, and Jellystack is ready for it. Both apps use the new Jellyfin 12 authentication and keep working with Jellyfin 10.x servers.

**📱 Android phone & tablet — 0.16.0**
- Sign in to Jellyfin 12.0+ with your password or Quick Connect
- Artwork, subtitles, and SyncPlay work on Jellyfin 12.0+
- Downloads queued before your server upgrade continue automatically
- Admins can now set a new password for a user instead of clearing it

**📺 Android TV, Google TV & Fire TV — 0.16.0 TV Beta 6**
- Household profiles with real Jellyfin names and avatars, optional PINs, separate settings, and My List
- Cinematic Home, Library, Search, and Discover: a large backdrop and trailer stage above compact rows of cards
- Buttons fade out while a trailer plays and come back as soon as you move to them
- Library: switch between cinematic Browse rows and a filterable All Titles grid
- Search Jellyfin and Seerr together, with voice search where your device supports it
- Jellyfin 12.0+ support for sign-in, Quick Connect, browsing, playback, subtitles, and SyncPlay
- Details open on Play or Resume, and one Back press leaves them
- More reliable remote navigation: the sidebar is reachable everywhere, rows start at the first card, and Back returns Home before an exit confirmation
- Cleaner focus highlight on buttons, plus fixes for overlapping screens, Seerr Discover, missing artwork, and subtitle placement

TV is still a beta. If something feels off, please share your device model, Android version, and the exact remote steps.
