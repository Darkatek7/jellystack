# Jellystack 0.16.0 — Jellyfin 12 compatibility

Jellystack 0.16.0 updates the Android phone and tablet app for Jellyfin 12.0+:

- Connect to Jellyfin 12.0+ with password login or Quick Connect.
- Watch videos with working artwork, subtitles, and SyncPlay on Jellyfin 12.0+.
- Continue queued downloads after upgrading your Jellyfin server.

The updated authentication also works with Jellyfin 10.x servers.

Download: https://github.com/Darkatek7/jellystack/releases/tag/v0.16.0
