# Jellystack TV 0.17.1 — easier household profiles and live watch state

A small update for Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV that makes shared TVs work better.

**Profiles**
- A new profile starts with the household's Jellyfin address, so the next person only signs in
- Every profile signs in to its own Seerr account
- With several profiles, Who's watching? appears on every start and after more than a minute away; quick trips to another app keep the current profile

**Watch state**
- Progress and watched marks from your other devices show up on Home while the app stays open
- Opening details refreshes that title

**Fixes**
- The year line is no longer cut off under long two-line titles on Home and Discover
- Audio and subtitle buttons in the player name languages in the app language

Please report your device model, OS version, and the exact remote steps if something looks wrong.
