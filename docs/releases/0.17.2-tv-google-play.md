# Google Play release details – Jellystack TV 0.17.2

## Release name

0.17.2 (33) – Playback and Fire TV polish

<en-GB>
Better playback on Fire TV. Real Direct Play with surround passthrough, and when Jellyfin transcodes it picks the best audio your TV supports. SRT, ASS and PGS subtitles show, PGS without burn-in. A second profile can sign in with a password, Quick Connect profiles join the household Seerr, and titles another profile requested show as requested. Rows reach the screen edges. Also: libraries open on their titles, SyncPlay pause, search editing, and focus fixes.
</en-GB>
