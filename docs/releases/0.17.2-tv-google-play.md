<en-GB>
Polish from a full Fire TV walkthrough. Books, music, playlists and collections open straight on their titles, and Back returns to the folder you came from. Seerr details load from My List and after a restart, with Retry if a load fails. Pause reaches SyncPlay groups. Fixed: a crash when reopening the app, the Search keyboard and menu, dialog focus, dark boxes in focused buttons, black frames before trailers and cluttered track names.
