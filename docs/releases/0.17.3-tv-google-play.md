# Google Play release details – Jellystack TV 0.17.3

## Release name

0.17.3 (34) – Faster start and stability

<en-GB>
A speed and stability update. Jellystack starts faster, uses less memory and is about a quarter of its old size. Play on a series or season starts the right episode. Discover no longer crashes on titles with several requests. The player opens straight away, Back cancels a start, and failed starts offer Retry. Short Wi-Fi drops keep the same stream. Expired sign-ins return to the sign-in screen, and SyncPlay reconnects. New: Settings > Diagnostics, stored only on your device.
</en-GB>
