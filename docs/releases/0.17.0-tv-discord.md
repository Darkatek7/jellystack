# Jellystack TV 0.17.0 — out of beta, with a new player and a full-screen home

Jellystack TV leaves the beta. 0.17.0 brings a redesigned player and a full-screen look for Home, Library, and Discover on Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV.

**Player**
- Clock and end time, a seek bar with chapters, segments, and the time left
- Left/Right with hidden controls scrubs with trickplay thumbnails (when your server generates them); Center pauses or resumes
- Chapters and episodes panels inside the player
- An up-next card with artwork when the credits start, with a countdown at the end
- Quality and subtitle size/background settings now apply

**Browsing**
- The home spotlight fills the screen, pages on its own, and lets a trailer take over while the remote rests
- Library and Discover share that look; each library ends with an All titles card
- Cards show the series, episode, time left, progress, and a watched check
- Details show 4K, HDR, Dolby Vision, Atmos, and surround badges, and seasons are reachable with the remote
- Every Play button asks whether to resume or start over
- Choose how long a card waits before its trailer starts

**Fixes**
- Search keeps the keyboard while typing
- The PIN pad has focus on start, and German labels are no longer cut off
- No connect-screen flash on start
- Seerr works right after connecting it, without restarting the app

Please report your device model, OS version, and the exact remote steps if something looks wrong.
