# Jellystack TV 0.18.0 — music, Live TV, and a much smoother Fire TV

The biggest TV release so far, for Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV.

**New places to go**
- Music: artist and album pages, track lists, shuffle, and a now-playing screen
- Live TV: a channel list with what is on now and next, when your Jellyfin server has channels
- Pages for people and collections, with Play all

**Speed**
- Home and Discover scroll far more smoothly on Fire TV
- Fewer images load while you move through a row

**Player**
- Picture size: Fit, Zoom, or Stretch
- Audio and subtitle delay while watching
- Optional refresh-rate matching. Fire TV offers apps fewer modes, so 24p titles stay as they are there
- DTS and TrueHD play without a server transcode when the device has no decoder for them. The app grows by about 6 MB
- More titles in Direct Play, and a fallback to the next decoder when one fails to start

**Android TV and Google TV**
- Continue Watching and Next Up in the Watch Next row. You can turn this off in Settings
- Jellystack library artwork as a screensaver, off until you pick it

**Polish**
- Rows remember your place when you come back to them
- Clearer loading, empty, and error screens
- Adding an account takes fewer presses, and the keyboard only opens after you press OK on a field

Please report your device model, OS version, and the exact remote steps if something looks wrong. Reports from Live TV setups with a real tuner, AV receivers, and Dolby Vision displays are especially welcome.
