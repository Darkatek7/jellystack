# Google Play release details – Jellystack TV 0.17.1

## Release name

0.17.1 (31) – Profiles and live watch state

<en-GB>
Profiles: a new profile starts with the household's Jellyfin address, and every profile signs in to its own Seerr account. With several profiles, Who's watching? appears on every start and after more than a minute away. Home and details pick up progress and watched marks from other devices while the app stays open. Fixes: the year line under long titles is no longer cut off, and audio and subtitle languages follow the app language.
</en-GB>
