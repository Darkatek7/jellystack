# Jellystack TV 0.17.2 — better playback and Fire TV polish

A fix-up release for Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV.

**Playback**
- Jellyfin now gets the full device profile and picks Direct Play or transcoding correctly
- Real Direct Play with surround passthrough (E-AC3, AC3)
- When transcoding, the best audio your TV supports; stereo only if no sound arrives
- SRT, ASS, and PGS subtitles show, PGS without server burn-in
- AV1 video stays untouched when only the audio needs a transcode

**Profiles and Seerr**
- A second profile can sign in with a password for an account already on the TV
- Quick Connect profiles join the household's Seerr automatically
- Titles another profile requested show as requested

**Libraries**
- Books, music, playlists, and collections open straight on their titles
- Back steps out of a folder to the card you came from
- The Playlists tile on Home opens the library

**Seerr**
- Details from My List or after a restart load fully, with Retry when they fail

**Player**
- Pause and resume reach your SyncPlay group
- Cleaner audio and subtitle names, one check mark per selected track, no empty chapter boxes

**Fixes**
- Rows cut off at the screen edges, focus rings, avatar outline, clipped text fields
- Search can be edited after submitting; the playback error dialog works with the remote
- Crash when reopening the app right after exiting
- Search keyboard and menu after closing the keyboard
- Dialogs now start on their default button
- Dark boxes in focused buttons, library images filling the screen, black frames before trailers, frozen trailers
- Server home sections follow the device language

Please report your device model, OS version, and the exact remote steps if something looks wrong.
