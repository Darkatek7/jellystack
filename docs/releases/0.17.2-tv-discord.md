# Jellystack TV 0.17.2 — polish from a full Fire TV walkthrough

A fix-up release for Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV.

**Libraries**
- Books, music, playlists, and collections open straight on their titles
- Back steps out of a folder to the card you came from
- The Playlists tile on Home opens the library

**Seerr**
- Details opened from My List or after a restart load genres, trailer, and request state
- A loading line while details load, and Retry when they fail

**Player**
- Pause and resume reach your SyncPlay group
- Cleaner audio and subtitle names, one check mark per selected track, no empty chapter boxes

**Fixes**
- Crash when reopening the app right after exiting
- Search keyboard and menu after closing the keyboard with Back
- Dialogs now start on their default button
- Dark boxes in focused buttons, library images filling the screen, black frames before trailers, frozen trailers
- Server home sections follow the device language

Please report your device model, OS version, and the exact remote steps if something looks wrong.
