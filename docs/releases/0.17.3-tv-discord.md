# Jellystack TV 0.17.3 — faster start and a lot more stable

A speed and stability release for Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV.

**Speed**
- Starts about a quarter faster on Fire TV
- Uses less memory
- The app is about a quarter of its old size

**Playback**
- Play on a series or season starts the right episode instead of a decoder error
- The player opens right away, Back cancels a start, failed starts offer Retry
- Short Wi-Fi drops keep the same stream instead of switching to a transcode
- The player closes when playback stops from outside it, no more black screen
- Remote and voice commands act like the on-screen buttons
- The screen saver can start after ten minutes of pause

**Crashes and sign-in**
- Discover no longer crashes on titles with several Seerr requests
- Home copes with duplicate sections, nameless items, and blank titles
- Marking a title watched while offline no longer crashes
- An expired Jellyfin session leads back to the sign-in screen

**SyncPlay**
- Reconnects after a connection drop
- Opens the player when the group starts a queue from another device

**New**
- Settings > Diagnostics shows the app version, the last crash, and a recent log. Nothing leaves the device.

Please report your device model, OS version, and the exact remote steps if something looks wrong.
