# Jellystack 0.16.0 TV Beta 4 — smoother browsing, stable previews

TV Beta 4 makes browsing recently added media and moving around Jellystack more responsive and predictable on Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV.

- Spotlight stays on the selected item until you move it, so trailers are not interrupted
- Trailer previews play only in the highlighted card, even when the same title appears in multiple rows
- Media opened from Home and Movies or Shows libraries no longer get stuck loading indefinitely
- Discover, Discover Movies, Discover TV Shows, and Upcoming rails use the same stable poster-card format
- D-pad focus and position restore consistently across rows, routes, and the sidebar

This is a beta release. Please share your TV or Fire TV model, OS version, and the exact navigation or playback steps if you find an issue.

Download: https://github.com/Darkatek7/jellystack/releases/tag/v0.16.0-tv-beta.4
