# Jellystack 0.16.0 TV Beta 4 — smoother browsing from Spotlight to sidebar

TV Beta 4 makes browsing recently added media and moving around Jellystack more responsive and predictable on Android TV, Google TV, and Fire TV.

- Spotlight now rotates through recently added movies and shows
- Trailer previews begin after the familiar short Spotlight pause
- Movies and Shows libraries open independently instead of waiting on unrelated Home requests
- Library loading no longer hangs indefinitely when a request stalls
- D-pad focus and position restore consistently across rows, routes, and the sidebar

This is a beta release. Please share your TV or Fire TV model, OS version, and the exact navigation or playback steps if you find an issue.

Download: https://github.com/Darkatek7/jellystack/releases/tag/v0.16.0-tv-beta.4
